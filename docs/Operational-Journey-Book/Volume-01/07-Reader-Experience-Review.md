# Reader Experience Review

| Reader | What works | Improvement incorporated |
|---|---|---|
| Hospital owner | Sees the full business chain and payment control | Executive summary and completion infographic lead with outcomes |
| Laboratory manager | Sees accession, routing, LIS, flags, verification and release | Laboratory sequence receives the deepest evidence coverage |
| Doctor | Sees previous reports and immutable prescription revision | v1/v2 comparison is explicit |
| Receptionist | Sees identity, appointment and queue hand-off | Reception chapter avoids technical terminology |
| Collection officer | Sees container and label controls | EDTA and serum evidence are shown independently |
| Technologist | Sees imports, values, flags and override protection | Normal, abnormal and blocked-edit screens are retained |
| Quality manager | Sees roles, audit, defects and corrections | Audit limitations and every J-01 defect remain visible |
| New employee | Can follow the journey chronologically | One screenshot per evidence page and consistent metadata |
| Investor | Sees an integrated operational platform | Chapter dividers and concise business explanations reduce detail overload |
| Software buyer | Can connect claims to browser proof | Every screenshot has a stable evidence ID and QC status |

## Confusing pages corrected

Long full-page captures were given extra image area and shorter captions. Near-duplicate screens were removed. Portal audit claims are labelled as read-only verifier evidence because there is no audit UI. Tenant deployment is described from proven J-01 readiness evidence without inventing a deployment screenshot.
